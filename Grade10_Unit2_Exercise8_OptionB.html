SAVING ENERGY AT HOME

Every day, families use energy for many different activities, such as cooking, lighting rooms, heating water, and using electrical appliances. Most people cannot stop using electricity completely, but they can change the way they use it. By developing simple energy-saving habits, families can reduce both their electricity bills and their impact on the environment.

Using too much electricity can cause environmental problems. [I] In many places, electricity is still produced by burning fossil fuels such as coal and gas. [II] This process releases greenhouse gases into the atmosphere and contributes to global warming. Therefore, reducing unnecessary electricity use can be an effective way to protect the environment.

Fortunately, saving energy at home does not require major changes. [III] People can start with small actions in their daily routines. [IV] For instance, they can turn off lights when leaving a room and unplug electronic devices when they are not being used. Families can also use energy-efficient appliances, which usually require less electricity to perform the same tasks.

These simple habits may seem unimportant, but their effects can become significant when many people practise them. By using energy more wisely, families can save money while helping to create a cleaner environment.
