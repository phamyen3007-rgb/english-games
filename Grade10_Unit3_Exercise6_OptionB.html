Music at School

Music is an important part of school life for many students. Some enjoy singing, while others prefer playing musical instruments. (1) ________. They can also make school more enjoyable and interesting.

Learning to play an instrument takes time and practice. At first, students may find it difficult to play a song correctly. (2) ________. After practising for several weeks, they usually become more confident and make fewer mistakes.

Music can also help students make new friends. When they join a school band or music club, they have opportunities to meet other students with similar interests. (3) ________. In this way, music can bring students closer together.

Taking part in a performance is another useful experience. Students may feel nervous before going on stage. (4) ________. When the audience claps for them, they often feel proud of what they have achieved.

Finally, music can help students relax after a busy day at school. Listening to a favourite song can reduce stress and improve their mood. (5) ________. For these reasons, music can have an important place in students' daily lives.
