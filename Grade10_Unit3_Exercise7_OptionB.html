Music and Teenagers

Music has become an important part of teenagers' daily lives. With smartphones and online platforms, young people can listen to almost any song whenever they want. (1) ________. Instead of waiting for their favourite songs to be played on the radio, they can now create their own playlists and discover new artists from around the world.

Music is also closely connected with teenagers' emotions. Many young people choose different types of music depending on how they feel. (2) ________. Others may prefer energetic songs when they need motivation before studying or doing exercise. For this reason, the same teenager may enjoy very different kinds of music at different times.

Music can also bring young people together. School concerts and music clubs give students opportunities to meet classmates who share similar interests. (3) ________. Working towards a common performance can therefore help students develop both friendships and teamwork skills.

However, listening to music is not always helpful. Some students enjoy having music on while doing their homework, but songs with strong beats or familiar lyrics may sometimes make it difficult to concentrate. (4) ________. Students therefore need to discover what works best for them rather than assuming that music always improves their study performance.

Another interesting effect of music is its ability to influence behaviour in public places. Some shops and restaurants carefully select the music they play to create a particular atmosphere. (5) ________. This shows that music is not simply entertainment; it can also affect the way people feel and behave.
