Music in the Park

Last Sunday, a free music festival called “Music in the Park” was held at Green City Park. The event attracted thousands of people, including teenagers, families, and tourists. Although the weather was quite hot, many people arrived early to find good places near the main stage. When the first band appeared, the crowd immediately began cheering and taking photos.

The festival featured several young singers and local bands. They performed different kinds of music, including pop, rock, and traditional Vietnamese songs. One of the most popular performances was given by a group of high school students. They played a well-known folk song using both traditional instruments and an electric guitar. The unusual combination surprised the audience at first, but soon everyone was clapping along with the music.

The most touching moment came when a young singer named Minh performed a song he had written for his mother. Before singing, he explained that his mother had always encouraged him to follow his dream of becoming a musician. His simple but emotional performance received loud applause from the audience. Some people even recorded the song and shared it online after the show.

[I] The festival was not only about entertainment. [II] The organizers also wanted to raise money for children who could not afford music lessons. [III] During the event, visitors could make donations at several booths around the park. [IV] By the end of the day, the festival had collected enough money to provide free music classes for more than 100 children.

The organizers were pleased with the success of the event and hoped to hold it again the following year. For many visitors, “Music in the Park” was more than just a chance to enjoy live performances. It showed how music could entertain people, bring them together, and support a good cause.
