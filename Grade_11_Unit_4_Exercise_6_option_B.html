Young people have an important role in strengthening cooperation among ASEAN communities. Regional programmes allow students to meet peers from different countries, discuss common concerns and work together on practical activities. These experiences can help them become more confident when communicating across cultures.

One important area is community service. Many youth projects focus on environmental protection, education and support for disadvantaged groups. (1) __________. As a result, students learn that successful volunteering requires careful planning, cooperation and a clear understanding of local needs.

Regional programmes can also challenge students' assumptions about people from other cultures. Participants may initially notice differences in food, language or communication styles. (2) __________. However, meaningful cooperation does not happen automatically. Participants need to listen carefully, respect different viewpoints and be willing to adjust their behaviour when misunderstandings occur. (4) __________. Without these qualities, even a well-designed programme may fail to create genuine connections among participants.

For this reason, ASEAN youth activities are valuable not simply because they bring students together. (5) __________. By combining cultural learning with practical cooperation, such programmes can help young people become more responsible members of both their local communities and the wider region.
